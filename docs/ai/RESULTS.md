# Measured results

Generated by `npm run ai:eval` on 2026-11-14 (Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz, 2 cores, Node v22.23.4).

All images here are synthetic, generated from a fixed seed with known ground truth. That makes the numbers exact and reproducible, and it is also their limit: they show the methods behave correctly on controlled input, not how they perform on real photographs and screenshots. See REPORT.md for what that does and does not establish.

## 1. Palette extraction

150 images per condition, each made of 2 to 5 colours at least ΔE00 22 apart, in known proportions.

- **mean ΔE00**: colour error between each true colour and the closest extracted one (under 1 is invisible, 2 to 3 is noticeable side by side). Capped at 50 per colour.
- **recall**: share of true colours recovered within ΔE00 5.
- **share error**: for recovered colours, how far off the reported proportion is, in percentage points.
- **extra colours**: how many swatches were returned beyond the true number.
- **exact count**: share of images where the number of swatches equals the true number of colours.
