# Privacy notice — DRAFT, not reviewed by a lawyer

This is a plain-language starting point describing what the software actually does. It must be reviewed and completed (who the data controller is, contact details, legal bases, retention periods, the host's and mail provider's names and locations) before Moodloom is offered to the public.

## What Moodloom stores

- **Account:** an email address and a password hash, if you choose to save your account; an optional display name.
- **Your work:** the links, files, notes, tags, briefs, sketches and brand kits you add.
- **Derived from your work:** thumbnails, colour palettes and a numeric description of each image's appearance, computed on Moodloom's server.
- **Usage counts:** how many direction notes you've requested this month, to apply plan limits.

Moodloom does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics, and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies.

## Where it goes

- Your data stays on Moodloom's server and database, with [hosting provider, region].
- **AI direction notes are optional and off by default.** If a project's owner switches them on, then each time a note is requested, a selection of that project's references (reduced images, titles, notes, tags) and its brief are sent to Anthropic to write the note. Nothing is sent otherwise.
- When you save a link, Moodloom's server fetches that page once to read its title and preview image.
- Password-reset emails are sent through [mail provider].

## Who can see your projects

Only you, people you invite, and anyone holding a view link you created (limited to the sections you switched on). You can turn a link off or remove a person at any time.

## Your control

- Download everything as a ZIP (Account → Your data).
- Delete any reference, project, or your whole account. Deletion removes the stored files as well.
- Sign out every device (Account → Sign out everywhere else).

## Browser extension

The extension reads the address and title of the tab you choose to save, and the address of an image or link you right-click and save. It does not read page content, browsing history, or anything on pages you don't save.
